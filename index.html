Your prices, your rules

Set up from your rate card

Everything the customer sees comes from one settings block. Send us your rate card and we fill it in.

  • Rates: base visit, per ft², per bedroom and bathroom, extra floors
  • Service types: standard, deep and move-out multipliers
  • Home factors: layout, last clean, clutter, pets, kids
  • Savings: which discounts are on, the cap, your minimum price
  • Brand: name, logo colors, guarantee copy, arrival windows
// Your rate card
rates: {
  base: 89, perSqft: 0.075,
  bedroom: 16, fullBath: 26, halfBath: 14,
  extraFloor: 20
},
types: {
  deep:    { mult: 1.6 },
  moveout: { mult: 1.9 }
},
addons: [
  { label: 'Inside oven',  price: 35 },
  { label: 'Inside fridge', price: 30 }
],
maxDiscountPct: 0.40,  // savings never exceed 40%
floorPrice: 120        // no visit below $120

Questions

Before you ask

Does it work with my current website?

Yes. It runs as its own page, so you can embed it with one line of code or link your existing "Get a quote" button to it. It works on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and custom sites.

Do I need a developer?

No. We set it up with your rates and branding. You paste one line, or send us your site login and we add it.

Can customers game the price?

The quote is a non-binding range, and customers are told the final price is confirmed before work starts. Condition, clutter and pets are asked about upfront, so a home that doesn't match its quote is easy to spot. Discounts can never go past the cap or your minimum price.

Where do bookings go?

Starter sends each request to your email with the full quote breakdown. Growth also writes it to your Google or Outlook calendar. Own it can connect to any CRM or scheduler that accepts web requests.

Can I change my prices later?

Yes. Send us the new numbers. Growth includes unlimited updates, and with Own it you edit one settings block yourself.

Is the 3D model slow on older phones?

It's built to be light. If a device can't show 3D, the price, breakdown and booking still work normally.
